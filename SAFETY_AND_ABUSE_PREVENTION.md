# Safety and Abuse Prevention

## Goal
Support fictional storytelling and design prototyping while reducing the risk that generated media is used to deceive, impersonate, harass, or fabricate evidence.

## Required controls
- Add a persistent, high-contrast `FICTIONAL MOCKUP` watermark to every exported image and every video frame.
- Do not offer a watermark-removal setting.
- Use generic social-media-inspired layouts rather than pixel-perfect copies of WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, or other services.
- Do not generate official logos, verified badges, platform system notices, or authenticity indicators that suggest a real platform issued the content.
- Use fictional starter names and handles.
- Warn users before uploading photos of people; recommend abstract avatars or properly licensed assets.
- Do not provide a feature that clones a named real person's profile or likeness.
- Do not connect to live social accounts or request passwords, one-time codes, cookies, or access tokens.
- Do not advertise the output as a genuine screenshot, leaked chat, or verified record.

## Disallowed workflows
- Removing or hiding the mockup label to make content appear authentic.
- Creating fake payment confirmations, bank/transfer receipts, account verification, login alerts, or official platform notices.
- Creating fabricated screenshots or videos intended to frame a real person, mislead an audience, or serve as false evidence.
- Impersonating a real person, public figure, company, or account.
- Creating realistic fake threats, confessions, endorsements, or statements attributed to a real person.

## Safe alternatives
- Offer clearly fictional names and sample data.
- Offer a “fictional scene” label for storyboards.
- Provide an export warning: “This file is a fictional mockup. Do not present it as an authentic record.”
- For education and design reviews, allow an optional project note describing the intended demo context.
- If a request is disallowed, explain the limitation briefly and offer a generic fictional version.

## Review checklist
- [ ] Watermark is visible at normal display size.
- [ ] Watermark appears on every video frame.
- [ ] Watermark cannot be disabled in export settings.
- [ ] No real platform login, verification, or receipt screens are included.
- [ ] Imported project JSON cannot switch off safety controls.
- [ ] User-provided names do not automatically trigger real-person lookups.
